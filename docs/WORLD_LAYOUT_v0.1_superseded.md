# WORLD LAYOUT — "Saltcrown" (working name), v0.1 awaiting approval

Original design. Tile 96 × 96 m of sea; island about 70 × 55 m; highest point +24 m.

## Land form
A crescent of rock open to the south-east, wrapped around a shallow lagoon harbour. The crescent's tall western horn carries the village; the low eastern horn is a reef spit ending in the landmark.

**Landmark — The Comb.** Three leaning rock fins at the tip of the eastern spit, 9–14 m tall, with a rope-and-plank bridge strung between the two nearest and a beacon lantern on top. Visible from every camera; frames the harbour mouth; where storms hit first.

## Routes (all walkable; ladders are extras)
- **Harbour Stair** T1→T2: wide stone stair, the main artery, faces the default camera.
- **Switchback Lane** T2→T3→T4: ramped path with short stair flights; cart-width to T2 only.
- **Back Stair** T1→T3 behind the boat yard: steep timber stair (second route so no terrace is a dead end).
- **Crown Path** T4→T5.
- **Boardwalk** T1→stilt homes; **Reef Walk** T1→The Comb (awash in storms — closes).
- Ladders: net loft to quay, T3 garden to T2 roof terrace, Comb beacon.
Every building door sits on one of these. Each terrace has at least two exits.

## First build (M1–M3 scope)
3 hero homes (H01 quay cottage, H02 stacked store-house on the plaza, H03 hillside family house with balcony), fish market, one pier, boat yard shed, Harbour Stair, plaza, The Comb. Enough to judge the look and to prove navigation and one household before anything is multiplied.

## Default camera
Orthographic-feeling perspective (FOV 20°), yaw from the south-east so the lagoon is in front and terraces stack behind, pitch 32°. The Comb sits lower-right; the crown tree upper-left.

## Fishing grounds
A: lagoon mouth (shore and handline, small fish). B: reef edge east of The Comb (traps, rod). C: open water south (nets, best catch, exposed in bad weather).
